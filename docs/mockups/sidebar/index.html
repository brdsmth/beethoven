Left sidebar redesign: four directions

Static mockups of the left sidebar (Sidebar in web/src/main.tsx), built with the app's own colour, type and radius tokens. The main area is greyed out so the sidebar stands out. The data is made up.

Problems with today's sidebar these try to fix: you can't search, the primary nav is a row of three text links at the bottom, sessions that need you (failed, waiting for review) look like any other, the Active/Archived switch takes a whole row, and the team switcher and brand stack up and push the list down.

A. Refined: same shape, less clutter

smallest change
  • Brand and team switcher become one header: the team name is the title, the product name sits under it.
  • Search plus a ⌘K command palette to jump to a session, repository or page.
  • Active/Archived becomes tabs with counts. A new Running tab answers "what's working right now?"
  • Repository and project groups collapse and show counts. Child sessions nest under their parent.
  • The meta line puts the PR state ahead of the author.
  • Automations, Insights and Settings move up as icon rows. Account and sign out move into an avatar menu in the footer.

B. Inbox: sessions grouped by what needs you

changes the grouping
  • Sessions are grouped by state, not by repository. Needs you (failed, or done with a PR to review) is tinted and always first.
  • Then Running and Recent, the latter bucketed by Today, Yesterday and This week. The repository goes on the meta line.
  • Pinned sessions stay at the top whatever their state.
  • A "Group by" menu (state, repository, person) keeps today's repository grouping for people who prefer it.
  • Rows are one line with the time on the right, so about twice as many fit. The full title shows on hover.
  • Archive is a nav entry, not a toggle, alongside Automations and Insights.

C. Team rail plus session panel

for multi-team servers
  • A 56px rail shows teams as Slack-style marks, with a badge for running (blue) or failed (red) sessions. Switching teams takes one click instead of opening a menu.
  • "All" and "Workspace" sit at the top of the rail. Automations, Insights and Settings sit at the bottom as icons.
  • The panel next to the rail is only about the chosen team: its projects first, then its sessions.
  • On servers without teams the rail drops away and the panel is the whole sidebar, close to A.

D. Collapsible: a focus mode for long sessions

pairs with A, B or C
  • ⌘B or the header button collapses the sidebar to a 56px strip, leaving more room for the transcript and diffs.
  • The strip keeps New session, the running and recent sessions as tiles with status dots, and the nav icons. Hover shows the full title.
  • The setting is remembered (writePref). On narrow screens the drawer stays as it is today.

Suggested path

Ship A first. Most of it is CSS and moving things around inside the existing Sidebar component; search can filter the list on the client before there's a server endpoint. Add D's collapse toggle next, since it's self-contained. Try B's "Needs you" section as an optional "Group by" mode before making it the default. Keep C in mind for when servers routinely have several teams.
